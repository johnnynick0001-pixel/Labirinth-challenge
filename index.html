🎮 The Labyrinth Maze

A Challenging Maze Adventure Game

📋 Game Description

Play with a blue ball through a maze, stealing items while avoiding enemies!

👾 Characters

🔵 Blue Ball (You)
🟡 Yellow Defenders
🔴 Red Guards
🟣 Purple Thieves

🎯 Objective

✅ Collect items while playing as the blue ball
⚠️ Avoid yellow defenders
🚨 Stay away from red guards
💔 Watch out for purple thieves (they steal your items!)

🎮 Controls

Use arrow keys or WASD to move your blue ball

Click "Start Game" to begin your adventure!
